# E-Commerce Store Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project involves end-to-end data cleaning, analysis, statistical evaluation, and interactive dashboard creation for an E-commerce store dataset. The analysis spans multiple dimensions including customer demographics, product catalogs, store locations, and sales transactions.

---

## Dataset Structure & Interconnected Tables
The dataset consists of four main tables:
1. **Customer_Dim**: Demographic and location details for 500 customers (including loyalty tiers).
2. **Product_Dim**: Catalog of 100 products categorized across multiple segments (Electronics, Sports, Home, Clothing, Beauty) with pricing and stock levels.
3. **Store_Dim**: Information on 20 retail locations categorized by geographic region, city, and format (flagship, outlet, online).
4. **Sales_Fact**: Transactional log capturing 2,000 orders linked to customers, products, and stores, complete with order dates, quantities, unit prices, discounts, payment methods, and total sales amounts.

---

## Key Analytical Framework & Findings
The project is organized into four core analytical pillars:

1. **Descriptive Analysis (What Happened?)**
   - Total Revenue: **₹21,74,394.70** across **4,953** units sold (**2,000** total orders).
   - Top Categories: **Sports** (₹5,42,010.17 / 1,241 units) and **Electronics** (₹5,04,311.35 / 1,165 units) lead the marketplace.
   - Regional Performance: High-performing metropolitan hubs in the **South and East** generated the highest revenues.
   - Peak Year: **2024** marked the highest annual revenue baseline driven by seasonal and holiday peaks.

2. **Diagnostic Analysis (Why Did It Happen?)**
   - Revenue dips post-2024 were driven by lower promotional frequency and reduced off-peak marketing campaigns.
   - The West region lagged due to lower local brand visibility and fewer retail fulfillment points.

3. **Predictive Analysis (What Is Likely to Happen?)**
   - Historical seasonal trends project a **15% to 20% surge** in sales volume for high-demand categories (Sports and Electronics) in the upcoming quarter.

4. **Prescriptive Analysis (What Should We Do?)**
   - Reallocate surplus inventory from slower categories (e.g., Beauty) to high-demand fulfillment hubs.
   - Launch targeted cross-selling and bundle promotions to elevate lower-performing product segments.
   - Expand localized marketing efforts in underperforming regions like the West.

- **Excel Workbook**: Contains structured tables, data cleaning steps, XLOOKUP formulas, descriptive statistics via Analysis ToolPak, pivot tables, and interactive dashboards.
- **Visualizations**: Clustered column charts, trend graphs, pie charts, and forecast visualizations.
- **Executive Summary**: Concise storytelling narrative translating data insights into actionable business strategies.
